# Benchmark results

Generated by `tests/benchmark.py` on Linux-6.18.54.1-microsoft-standard-WSL2-x86_64-with-glibc2.39 (12 CPUs), all peers on loopback with uploads capped at 2 MB/s each. Median of 3 runs; piece size 512 KB.

## Flash crowd: 8 clients fetch a 8 MB file from one seed at once

| Partial sharing | Time until all finish (s) | Runs (s) |
|---|---:|---|
| off | 14.94 | 18.15, 14.44, 14.94 |
| on | 6.92 | 6.92, 6.71, 7.19 |

Partial sharing finishes the crowd 2.2x faster. Without it a downloader uploads only once it has the whole file; if the seed alone served everyone it would take 32 s. With it, every downloader uploads from its first piece on; the seed must still send each piece at least once, so the lower bound is 4 s.
