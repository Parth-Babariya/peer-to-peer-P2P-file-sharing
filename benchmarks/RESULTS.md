# Benchmark results

Generated by `tests/benchmark.py` on Linux-6.18.54.1-microsoft-standard-WSL2-x86_64-with-glibc2.39 (12 CPUs), all peers on loopback with uploads capped at 2 MB/s each. Median of 3 runs; piece size 512 KB.

| Seeders | Time (s) | Throughput (MB/s) | Speed-up | Runs (s) |

| 8 | 1.25 | 12.8 | 6.6x | 1.25, 1.25, 1.25 |

## Flash crowd: 8 clients fetch a 8 MB file from one seed at once

| Partial sharing | Time until all finish (s) | Runs (s) |
|---|---:|---|
| off | 32.11 | 32.11, 32.11, 32.11 |
| on | 11.95 | 13.20, 11.20, 11.95 |

Partial sharing finishes the crowd 2.7x faster. Without it the seed alone uploads 8 x 8 MB; the lower bound is 32 s. With it, every downloader also uploads; the seed must still send each piece at least once, so the lower bound is 4 s.
